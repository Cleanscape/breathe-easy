Common questions

How do I cancel Premium?

On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Breathe Easy. On Android: Google Play, your profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

I got a new phone. Where did my progress go?

Your quit data stays on the device and is not backed up to us. Premium comes back with Restore purchase on the Premium screen, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

I slipped. Do I lose my streak?

Each smoke-free week earns a slip shield. A shield absorbs one slip and your streak carries on.

Is this medical advice?

No. Breathe Easy supports your quit and is not a medical treatment. Talk to your doctor or a local stop-smoking service for help with nicotine replacement or medication.
