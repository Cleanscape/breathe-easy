Breathe Easy privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Breathe Easy is made by Vaughan. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the Breathe Easy app on iPhone, Android or the web.

What stays on your device

Your quit date, cigarettes per day, pack price, slips, cravings beaten, badges and savings goal are stored only on your device (or in your browser). We do not send them to our servers, and we have no account system.

Purchases

If you start a Premium trial or subscription, the payment is handled by Apple, Google or (on the web) Stripe. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, but not your quit data. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Analytics and ads

Breathe Easy has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Health information

Health milestones come from the World Health Organization and the NHS. Breathe Easy supports your quit and is not a medical treatment.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Start over, or delete the app, to remove everything stored on your device.

Children

Breathe Easy is not directed at children under 13.
